# Steering — presentation (Costume Contest)

Haunt is entered in Costume Contest: the spooky interface IS the game,
and Design is a scored criterion.

- **Readable at a glance.** Sanity, ectoplasm and time-to-dawn are
  always on screen, always legible. No hidden state decides a run.
- **Every scare is a small performance.** Sound, light and animation
  fire together.
- **Haunted, not gory:** flickering candlelight, fog at the edges,
  creaking doors, portraits whose eyes follow, dust, night-to-dawn sky.
- **Five minutes, complete arc:** calm investigation, rising dread,
  panicked rout, then dawn. No run outstays its welcome.
- **Assets:** free-license or generated only, no third-party trademarks
  or copyrighted music. Record every asset's source in the repo.
- **Demo video:** under 3 minutes, shows the game working end-to-end,
  public on YouTube.
